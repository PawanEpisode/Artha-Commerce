## 0. What already exists, and what this document adds

Browser push is **not new**. X-01 and X-01.1 specify it and the code has it: service worker, Web Push with VAPID, device registry, inbox, quiet hours, daily cap, scheduled jobs through QStash, and these push events: `timer_end`, `break_over`, `stopwatch_long`, `goal_reached`, `streak_at_risk`, `daily_nudge`, `revision_due`, `exam_milestone`, `weekly_summary`, `daily_digest`. Clicking a push opens the deep link and reuses an open tab (FR-N6). On Android the alert can carry buttons (FR-N12). The floating timer pop-out (F-01.3) is built too.

So there are two separate questions, and this document answers both:

1. **Why do you only see in-app notifications?** Most likely because the pipeline is not live for you yet, not because it is missing. `docs/BUILD-TRACKER.md` still lists X1 (the QStash callback host had a typo, so every callback returned 404), X2 (device matrix not recorded) and X4 (rollout percentages of `push_notifications`, `keep_awake`, `floating_timer` not recorded). Phase 0 below is a checklist to confirm this on your own account before any code is written. It could not be verified from the documents alone.
2. **What is missing?** Two things.
   - **The "Still studying?" check for the stopwatch is in the page only.** Today the page decides when the student has gone quiet and then asks (`TrackerContainer`, `POST tracking/stopwatch/idle/`). A student in another tab or another application is never asked, because the page is not running its check or is throttled. The stopwatch keeps counting until the 3-hour `stopwatch_long` push (FR-N, W3.2), and a student who ignores nothing still ends up with hours of false time. There is also no message afterwards saying the timer was paused.
   - **Work that finishes in the background tells nobody.** Notes AI summary, page reading, OCR, flattened PDF export, and Recall AI suggestions run as jobs and the result appears only on the screen that started them. No module except `focus` and `tracking` calls `notifications.services.notify`.

The Pomodoro "away" case is already designed: a round that ends while the student is elsewhere sends `timer_end` (with away wording), and the click lands on `/app/focus` where `AwayDialog` asks whether the round counts. This document keeps it, adds action buttons on desktop Chrome and Edge, and makes the click path testable (FR-A12 to FR-A14).

## 1. Problem and goal

A student who starts a timer and leaves the tab is invisible to the product. Either the timer runs on and records time they did not study, or it stops silently and they find a gap later. A product that cares about honest study time has to reach the student where they are: the lock screen, the dock, the Windows notification area. Linear, Cursor and Claude do this: a short alert, one tap to act, and the tap lands on the exact screen.

**Goal.** When a timer needs the student and the app is not in front of them, a push arrives within seconds with a one-tap answer. When they do nothing, the timer settles by the same honest rule as today (pause at the last moment they were seen) and they are told. When a long job finishes, they are told once, with a link to the result.

## 2. Users and scenarios

1. **Riya, CA Inter, stopwatch.** She starts the stopwatch on Taxation, switches to the ICAI PDF in another window and then to WhatsApp. After her idle limit (10 minutes, her setting) a push says "Still studying Taxation? Pause in 2 minutes if you do not answer". She taps "Still studying" on the notification. Nothing opens, the stopwatch continues. Had she ignored it, the stopwatch would have paused at the last moment she was active and a second push would say so.
2. **Arjun, CMA, Pomodoro.** A 25 minute round ends while he is on a video call. A push says "Round 2 done"; Chrome on his laptop shows "Start break" and "Pause" buttons. He taps "Start break" and the break starts without opening the app. (Pomodoro away handling already exists; the new part is the buttons on desktop.)
3. **Meera, CS Executive, notes.** She uploads a 600 page scanned PDF and goes to cook. When OCR finishes she gets "OCR finished: Company Law module" and the click opens the reader at that document.

## 4. Scope

**In scope**

- Phase 0: confirm the existing pipeline works end to end on a real account and device (checklist, no code).
- Server-driven "Still studying?" check for the stopwatch, with push, buttons, a settle step and a "paused" follow-up.
- Action buttons on desktop Chromium (Chrome and Edge on Windows, macOS and Linux), in addition to Android.
- Click landing: the page opens on the right screen with the right prompt already showing.
- A `results` category with four events from `notes` and `recall` jobs, through the public `notify()` function.
- Settings: one switch for the idle alert (reusing `idle_minutes`, `0` means off) and the `results` category switch.

**Out of scope (later)**

- Pop-out or desktop companion changes (F-01.3 and P5 are separate).
- A mid-round presence check for the Pomodoro. Presence is judged at the end of a round (PRD B, `PRESENCE_WINDOW_SECONDS`); changing that rule is a product decision (Q-A3).
- Email, WhatsApp and SMS for these events.
- Notifications from modules that are not built (F-06 results, F-07 evaluation, F-13 plan). They keep their catalogue entries and use `notify()` when they arrive.
- Safari declarative push (parked as X6).

## 5. User flows

### 5.1 Stopwatch idle check (server-driven)

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
  participant P as Page (visible tab)
  participant S as tracking (stopwatch)
  participant N as notifications (job runner)
  participant D as Student device (push)
  P->>S: start or resume, then alive and active pings
  S->>N: stopwatch_changed (version, last_active_at)
  N->>N: plan stopwatch_idle at last_active_at + idle_minutes
  Note over N: fires; page not seen for 90 s, so no in-page prompt is possible
  N->>S: judge (read only): still running, same version, quiet since last_active_at
  alt student active meanwhile
    N->>N: reschedule to the new last_active_at + idle_minutes
  else idle
    N->>S: request_idle_check (sets idle_pending, idle_prompted_at)
    N->>D: push "Still studying?" with Still studying and Pause
    N->>N: plan stopwatch_idle_end at prompted_at + 120 s
  end
  alt student taps Still studying
    D->>S: answer_idle(still_studying) through one-time token
    S->>N: stopwatch_changed, new plan
  else no answer
    N->>S: settle_idle (existing rule: pause at last active moment)
    N->>D: push "Paused at 4:12" with Resume
  end
```

### 5.2 Edge cases every wave handles

| Case | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| A visible tab is open and sending `alive` | No push. The page asks as it does today and marks `prompted`; the server still plans the settle job so a tab that closes mid-prompt is covered |
| Student answers in the page after the push was sent | The push is replaced or dismissed through the shared `tag`; the settle job finds `idle_pending = false` and does nothing |
| Stopwatch paused, stopped or replaced before the job fires | Job skipped (`paused`, `gone`, `changed`), nothing sent |
| Idle push is more than 2 minutes late at send time | Dropped as `suppressed: stale` (the answer window would already be over) |
| Quiet hours | The check still goes out (priority 0): the student started the timer and the settle rule pauses it either way. The "paused" follow-up is priority 1 and obeys quiet hours; it is always in the inbox |
| No push device, permission denied, flag off | Nothing is sent. The settle rule still pauses the stopwatch after the page-side or lazy rule exactly as today. The inbox row is still created |
| Student has two devices | Both get the push, one shared `tag`; the first answer wins, the second tap returns `already` |
| Idle alert switched off (`idle_minutes = 0`) | No job is planned. The in-page prompt is also off, as today |
| Student offline when the push arrives | The push service holds it up to its time-to-live (set to 120 s for this event); after that it is dropped and the settle job pauses and sends the follow-up |

## 8. Data and permissions

No new table. Changes are in the ERD: one column and two check-constraint widenings in `notifications`, two job kinds, two events, one category. `tracking` gains two service functions and no column. ## 9. API surface

| Method and path | Change |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /api/v1/notifications/actions/` | Accepts the new actions `still_studying` and `pause` for a stopwatch token; answers `done`, `already`, `stale` or `needs_app` as today |
| `POST /api/v1/notifications/internal/jobs/{id}/fire/` | Handles `stopwatch_idle` and `stopwatch_idle_end`; a `Reschedule` verdict answers 200 and re-queues |
| `GET /api/v1/notifications/categories/` | Includes `results` |
| `GET /api/v1/tracking/stopwatch/` | No change in shape; `idle_pending` and `idle_prompted_at` already exist |

Internal, no HTTP: `tracking.services.request_idle_check(user_id, client_id, version)` and `tracking.services.settle_idle(user_id, client_id)`; `notifications.services.notify(...)` is called from `notes` and `recall` job handlers (through the notifications barrel, never by importing its models).

## 11. Non-functional requirements

- **Latency.** Idle push within 5 seconds of its fire time at p95; the whole answer window is 120 seconds, so a push later than 120 s is dropped.
- **Cost.** Each running stopwatch adds at most one pending queue message at a time (reschedule re-uses the row). Estimate at 1,000 students studying at once: about 1,000 messages per idle spell, well under the QStash plan limit; confirm in Q-A5.
- **Reliability.** The settle rule never depends on a push. The sweep (every minute) fires overdue `stopwatch_idle` and `stopwatch_idle_end` jobs as the safety net, so a lost queue message delays the pause by about one minute and never loses time.
- **Accuracy.** Pause time stays "last active moment", unchanged from F-01.2 FR-7. Push buttons change who answers, not how time is counted.
- **Accessibility.** Notification text is plain and short; every action also exists in the page (keyboard operable); settings rows follow the four-theme and WCAG 2.2 AA rules.
- **Privacy.** DPDP consent is the existing alerts consent (FR-N26); no new purpose beyond study-related alerts the student chose.
